## Editing and privacy

Excerpt boundaries are trimmed from the original call. Internal pauses, turn timing and speech are preserved. Audio is encoded as a mono MP3 and identifying container metadata is removed. No silence compression, synthetic replacement or stitched responses are used.

No names, email addresses, phone numbers, account identifiers or calendar details are spoken in these excerpts. The platform transcript has been reviewed for publication. Recordings are copyrighted portfolio evidence; they are not offered under a redistribution licence.
