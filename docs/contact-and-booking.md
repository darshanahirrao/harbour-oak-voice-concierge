# Contact and booking procedure

This document describes the native agent's intended behaviour. It is not executable policy or a guarantee that a language model cannot violate a step.

## Confirm exact contacts

For an appointment, collect the caller's full name, exact email and preferred callback number. Do not assume the preferred number is the caller ID.

Email confirmation preserves dots, underscores, hyphens, plus tags, repeated letters, digits and the domain ending. Separate the local part and domain when needed. Clarify only uncertain characters, then read back the complete address for explicit approval. Any correction invalidates previous approval.

After two unsuccessful repairs, switch to a preferred callback request for human verification. A landline caller can use this route without receiving a text message. No invite goes to an uncertain address.

## Agree a valid appointment

The demonstration offers 20-minute video consultations on future weekdays, fully within 10:00 to 16:00 Europe/London. The latest start is 15:40. Resolve relative dates against current time and apply the date-specific London offset. Confirm the full date, time and timezone with the caller.

Recap the useful project notes and ask for permission to send the demo invitation with those notes. Agreement to a slot or approval of an email read-back alone is not permission to create an invitation.

## Check, create, verify

1. Obtain confirmed name, exact email and preferred callback number.
2. Agree a valid slot and the brief to include.
3. Obtain explicit permission to send the invitation.
4. Check availability again for that exact interval.
5. Create one clearly labelled demo event.
6. Require a successful event identifier, confirmed status and attendee/start/end matching the agreement before reporting success.

Changes to the email, time, notes or consent require the affected details to be approved again and availability checked again. Notes contain approved project facts; they omit callback digits, sensitive data and arbitrary caller instructions. The event title and description identify the demonstration.

## Failures and uncertainty

An unavailable slot, invalid hours, refused consent or failed availability check prevents creation. A creation timeout is an unknown outcome: retain it for review, do not automatically retry and do not claim success or definite failure. Missing inbox mail also does not justify creating another event.

Existing-event changes and cancellations require operator review in this demonstration. No unsupported update or cancellation action is claimed.
