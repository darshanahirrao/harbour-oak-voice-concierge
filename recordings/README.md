# Recordings and transcripts

## Complete scripted conversations

Two full conversations produced on **2 October 2026** with Eleven v4, using Lucy (Maya) and Roger (caller). Restrained expression tags shape the delivery. These are **scripted two-voice simulations**, not recordings of autonomous agents speaking, real customers or a live telephone connection. They illustrate the configured inbound workflow.

Calendar results and operator-review outcomes in these scripts are mocked. No live tool runs, event creation or callback action occurs when producing or playing this audio. The original synthetic timing is retained; it is not a measurement of live call latency or interruption handling. The MP3 audio stream is preserved at its native 44.1 kHz / 128 kbps quality, with identifying container metadata removed.

Names are fictional. The email uses the example.com domain. Spoken callback numbers come from the [Ofcom London range reserved for drama](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/phones-and-broadband/phone-numbers/numbers-for-drama?language=en). They are sample data, not a callable demo line.

### From a lighting idea to a consultation

[Play the complete conversation](maya-lighting-consultation.mp3)

**Outcome in the script:** Demo booking confirmed in the script.

**Maya:** Hi, Harbour and Oak. I'm Maya, the AI design concierge. This demo is recorded. How can I help?

**Caller:** Hi! I like my kitchen, but the lighting's awful. I'd love soft light in the evening and something brighter when I'm cooking.

**Maya:** Separate ambient and under-cabinet lighting would give you both, with independent controls. Our illustrative Focus and Glow concept is six hundred and fifty pounds, before installation.

**Caller:** That sounds good. What would I lose with the cheaper option?

**Maya:** Soft Glow is three hundred and fifty pounds for ambient lighting. It doesn't include bright task lighting, so Focus and Glow is the closer match.

**Caller:** Right, let's explore that. Could I book a video consultation on Monday, the fifth of October, at one, London time?

**Maya:** Of course. It's a twenty-minute demo consultation. What's your full name and email for the invitation?

**Caller:** Alex Morgan. It's alex dot morgan plus lighting underscore seven, at example dot com.

**Maya:** Alex dot morgan, plus lighting, underscore seven, at example dot com. Is that complete address exactly right?

**Caller:** Ah, sorry. Eight, not seven. Everything else is right.

**Maya:** No problem. Alex dot morgan, plus lighting, underscore eight, at example dot com. Is that exact address correct?

**Caller:** Yes, that's correct.

**Maya:** And what's the best number for a callback? It can be different from the number you're calling on.

**Caller:** Use my other number. Plus four four, two zero, seven nine four six, zero nine five eight.

**Maya:** Plus four four, two zero, seven nine four six, zero nine five eight. Have I got that right?

**Caller:** You have. And please tell the designer I only want lighting, not a whole new kitchen.

**Maya:** Absolutely. Monday, the fifth of October, one p.m. London time, for twenty minutes. Notes: lighting only, soft evening light, brighter cooking light and separate controls. Shall I send the demo invitation with those notes?

**Caller:** Yes, please send it.

**Maya:** I'll check that time and arrange the demo invitation.

**Maya:** Your demo consultation is confirmed for Monday, the fifth of October, from one to one twenty p.m. London time. The invitation was requested with your lighting notes.

**Caller:** Brilliant. Should I have anything ready?

**Maya:** A couple of room photos or measurements would be helpful, if you have them. No payment is needed for the demo consultation.

**Caller:** Lovely. Thanks, Maya. Bye!

**Maya:** You're welcome, Alex. Have a lovely afternoon. Goodbye!
